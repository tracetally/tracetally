Your LLM bill tells you the total. tracetally tells you why.

Process

How it works

Four steps, all from data you export. Nothing needs to sit in your request path.

  1. Send exports

    Share read-only usage exports, traces (Langfuse, LangSmith, or OpenTelemetry), and redacted prompt templates. A mutual NDA is available before you send anything.

  2. I analyse

    Spend is broken down by model, feature and day, then reviewed against a nine-area methodology: routing, token bloat, caching, batching, RAG context, agent loops and retries, eval coverage, observability, and latency.

  3. Findings report, ranked by dollars saved

    Every finding comes with an estimated monthly saving or reliability gain, the effort to fix it, and the quality risk, so you know what to do first.

  4. Walkthrough call

    A readout walks your team through the plan. Quick Review includes a 30-minute call; Full Audit and Implementation include a 60-minute session.

Deliverables

What you get

A plan both engineering and finance can read. Scope follows the tier you book; the Full Audit is listed in full below.

  • A written report: 4–6 pages for a Quick Review, or 15–25 pages for a Full Audit
  • Spend breakdown by provider, model, endpoint or feature, and time
  • Findings ranked by estimated monthly saving or reliability gain, with effort, quality risk and confidence
  • For a Full Audit: a savings and reliability scorecard, and a prioritised 30/60/90-day roadmap
  • A walkthrough call (30 or 60 minutes, by tier), recorded on request
  • Two weeks of async Q&A by email after a Full Audit readout
  • For Implementation: merge-ready pull requests, a before/after report, a runbook and a handover call

Each finding includes

Evidence from your exports, an estimated monthly dollar impact, the effort to fix it, the quality risk, and a confidence note.

Methodology checklist

Model choice and routing, prompt and token bloat, caching, batching, RAG retrieval and context size, agent loops and retries, eval coverage, observability gaps, and rate limits and latency.

What stays out of scope

Production deploys (your team deploys), on-call support, vendor negotiations, model fine-tuning, security or compliance certification, and any access to production systems or raw customer personal data. Work beyond the agreed list is a change request at the hourly rate.

Data handling

Privacy

The work uses exports you share. A mutual NDA is available. No access to production is needed.

Exports, not production

Analysis is done offline from usage CSVs, trace exports and redacted prompts you provide. Live traffic is not intercepted, and production systems are not accessed. For Implementation, write access is limited to feature branches and pull requests that your team reviews and merges.

NDA first, if you want one

A mutual non-disclosure agreement is available before you share data. If you already have an NDA or data-processing agreement, that can be used after review.

Redact personal data

Please redact or pseudonymize end-user personal data before sharing, and share prompt or response content only where it is needed and already redacted. Unexpected personal data is deleted and you are notified.

Stored privately, then deleted

Data is kept only on encrypted personal storage, is not uploaded to third-party AI services for training, and is not shared with third parties. All client data and copies are deleted within 14 days of the final deliverable, or earlier on request, with written confirmation.

Questions

FAQ

What data do I need to share?

For a Quick Review: a 30-day usage export from one provider console, up to five production prompt templates with customer data redacted, and a short note on which features call which models. For a Full Audit, add usage exports for each provider, a trace export (Langfuse, LangSmith, or OpenTelemetry spans), prompts for the top endpoints by spend, and an optional read-only look at the relevant code paths. A short kickoff call with the tech lead is part of the Full Audit.

How is our data handled?

A mutual NDA is available first. Access is read-only, except for feature-branch pull requests in the Implementation tier. Please redact personal data before sharing. Data stays on encrypted personal storage, is analysed with offline tooling, is not used to train anything, and is deleted within 14 days of the final deliverable, with written confirmation.

Do you need access to production?

No. Production systems are not accessed, and live traffic is not intercepted. Everything starts from exports you share. In Audit + Implementation, pull requests are opened on feature branches; your team reviews, merges and deploys.

How long does an engagement take?

Quick Review takes about 7–10 days, a Full Audit about 3–4 weeks, and Audit + Implementation about 8–10 weeks, counted from when the data is received.

Who is this a good fit for?

Teams with an LLM feature in production (chat, RAG, agents, or extraction) and some form of logging or tracing. The usual fit is monthly LLM API spend of about $1,500 to $50,000 (₹1.5L to ₹45L). Below about $1,000 a month, the fee rarely pays back, and that will be said plainly on the intro call.

How does payment work?

Quick Review is 100% upfront. Full Audit is 50% at kickoff and 50% on delivery of the report. Audit + Implementation is 40% at kickoff, 30% at the audit report, and 30% at handover. International clients are invoiced in USD; Indian companies in INR. Prices exclude applicable taxes (including GST for Indian clients).

Will model or routing changes put quality at risk?

Routing recommendations are checked with an offline replay on a small, redacted sample, and against your evals when you have them. Where there are no evals, a small golden set can be part of the plan. Some changes, such as enabling caching or moving asynchronous jobs to a batch API, do not change model outputs.

What if we need work beyond the agreed scope?

Work beyond the agreed fix list is a change request at $60/hr / ₹4,000/hr. Production deploys stay with your team. The Quick Review fee is credited in full against a Full Audit booked within 30 days.
